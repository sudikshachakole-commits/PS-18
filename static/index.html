ℹ️ About — PS-18
AI Grant & Funding Finder for Startups
🎯 Problem Statement #18

Developing an AI-based Grant and Funding Finder for startups solves the challenge of navigating dispersed, complex funding opportunities. This system aggregates 15 curated grant schemes and matches them against 80 startup profiles using a multi-signal scoring engine. IBM Granite generates personalised application drafts and eligibility analyses.

🏗️ Architecture
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python)
  • AI Engine: IBM Granite 13B Instruct v2
  • Platform: IBM watsonx.ai (Cloud Lite)
  • Matching: Multi-signal scoring (stage, sector, DPIIT, deadline, funding)
  • Datasets: grant_schemes_15.json · startup_profiles_80.csv
  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS
